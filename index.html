Your mix

The starting mix. Make it yours.

    Score = base + 20 × weighted topic preference

    How your mix works ↗
    weight = 1 + (time ÷ cap) × (max − 1)
    fade = max(0, 1 − age ÷ fade days)
    signal = less/more × weight × fade
    score = base + 20 × topic mean(signal)

    Time in view is a rough interest signal, not proof of attention. We keep your longest uninterrupted view, capped at 10 seconds by default. Counting stops when the post leaves view, you switch posts, or this page loses focus. Everything stays on this device.

    Time alone never requests more. More boosts a topic; Less lowers it. At the default 10-second cap, either answer has 2× weight. The feeling selector chooses which More answers count: liked, disliked, neutral, or both (all feelings). Less always counts. A centered feed answer has no effect.

    Each real post starts at 70. We average these signals for each source topic. New posts inherit that topic's score; we do not predict how you will feel about an unseen post. Higher scores rank first; ties keep the original mixed order.

    Preferences fade evenly toward neutral: half strength halfway through the selected period, no effect at its end. A new gesture renews that answer. Viewing, reloading, and reach votes do not. Your original choices stay saved.

    Wider reach is a separate local opinion. No signal is sent to Bluesky. Click counts and Bluesky likes do not enter the score; public source feeds have their own selection rules. This demo never posts, shares, reports, or moderates anything.
